# Zotero

## What is it and why does it help? 

[Zotero](https://github.com/zotero/zotero) is a free tool to collect, organize, cite and share research sources (Zotero, n.d.-f). It was created at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University (Zotero, n.d.-a). In journamlism, work involves a lot of literature, so a well-ordered bibliography that I can always return to is essential. When I read a study in my browser, the Zotero Connector saves it directly into my research project and gives me a short citation for my text. I can save it under my chapter and access it again without downloading and sorting PDFs myself. At the end, I get the full bibliography in the citation style I need.

### The five checks

The licence is the AGPLv3: I can use, study, change and share the software, and if I modify it and offer it to others, I must share my changes under the same licence (Zotero, n.d.-c). The latest version, 10.0.8, came out on 6 November 2026 (Zotero, n.d.-e), and the latest commit was on 9 November 2026. Commits are frequent, so this changes quickly. 

About 90 people have contributed (Zotero, n.d.-f). 

Bug reports go to the Zotero forums, and GitHub issues are only for confirmed bugs (Zotero, n.d.-c), so the 1,413 open issues (Zotero, n.d.-b) say little about support. Questions are answered, in October 2026 for example, there was a discussion about a cell update (Zotero, n.d.-b). 

Zotero began with funding from the Mellon Foundation, the Institute of Museum and Library Services and the Sloan Foundation (Zotero, n.d.-a). Today, storage subscriptions pay for it: 300 MB are free, paid plans cost $20, $60 or $120 per year (Zotero, n.d.-d).

### Would I use it for my jourmalism thesis?

I would rely on Zotero for a thesis, because it is active, non-profit and funded. I could also leave it, since it exports to formats like BibTeX and RIS (Wikipedia contributors, n.d.). Still, it would be bad if the system broke during my research, because all my sources are stored there and my research depends on them.
